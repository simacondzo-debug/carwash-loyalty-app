# THE CARWASH launch check

Run this on one customer phone and the owner device before promoting the app.

1. Open the customer link on mobile data and confirm the homepage shows the service price, booking button, WhatsApp button, and map directions.
2. Open the Menu tab and confirm wash services and prices appear. If the CSV cannot load, confirm the fallback menu still appears.
3. Create one test customer, accept the privacy note, and confirm a customer code appears.
4. Make one test booking. On the owner device, confirm it appears in Bookings queue and assign a queue number.
5. Mark the test booking complete. Reopen the customer app and confirm the booking update appears once.
6. Verify one paid wash on the owner device. Reopen the customer app and confirm a stamp appears once.
7. Send test feedback from the customer app. Reply from the owner area and confirm the customer can see the reply.
8. Export an owner backup and keep it somewhere private.
9. Check the owner link on a second device. It should show the owner lock rather than customer records.

For daily use, keep customer data private, only tick WhatsApp approval when the customer agrees, and use the owner backup after material changes.
